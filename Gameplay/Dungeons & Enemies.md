
# Design Rule

No 2 dungeons should be exactly the same, they can be similar in design or layout but never the exact same

# Layouts

- Floors: Go deeper down fight harder enemies and bosses (multiple per dungeon)
- Straight Forward: Not a direct path but obvious where to go
- Branching paths: multiple routes some of which lead to separate bosses and maybe collapsing back after into one final boss
