
# Party

Party will consist of 4 players maybe more for harder/late game content, players can play any combination of classes but some dungeons will be hard without certain class types (tank, healer). Players will also be given a bonus for mixing between the pillars 

# Combat Style 

For the 2.5D and 8bit versions of the game i will do it as turn-based combat as it makes it easier but for the 3D version i will make it real time combat. Both versions with have AAs and abilities with GCD and some abilities that are not on GCD (GCD stands for Global CoolDown meaning they share a cooldown)

# Levelling & XP

Players will get XP after any boss within a dungeon (only full bosses no mini bosses) and they will level from 1 to 50, levelling will give the higher base stats, access to higher level dungeons and new gear 

# Gear

Gear will give both defence and stats with there being a set for each pillar with a higher drop rate and the a specific set for each subclass with a lower drop chance starting within the 2nd dungeon

# Enemy Style 

Do i do the enemies with set roles (Tank, Healer, Ranged as example) or do i keep it like FFXIV and just make the AA the party to death

# PvP

The pillars hold trials pitting there best warriors against each other, the winning party takes home a small fortune given to them by both of the contesting pillars
