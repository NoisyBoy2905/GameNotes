
Magic is one of the main classes within the game, each of its sub classes are:

- Alchemist - Ranged, various potions with different effects, AOE and DOT
- [[Sorcerer]] - Ranged, many spells across damage types and effects
- Druid - Ranged, can change itself and others into different forms 
- Hexblade - Tank, mix of melee and ranged
