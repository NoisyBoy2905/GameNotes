
Religion is one of the main classes within the game, each of its sub classes are:

- [[Paladin]] - Melee Tank. only does slashing and holy damage
- Cleric - Ranged healer with high damage holy magic 
- Oracle - Ranged support, supplies buffs and shield to allies and debuffs to enemies
- Fanatic - Melee, applies buffs to allies based on damage dealt to enemies
