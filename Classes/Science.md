
Science is one of the main classes within the game, each of its sub classes are:

- Artificer - Crafter/Fighter, can craft both melee and range weapons to use
- Chimera - Summoner, combines creatures into monsters they control
- [[Medic]] - Backliner, believes that all life matter but respects their teammates, will only fight back if necessary
- Synth - Augments themself, tank or DPS depending on slotted augments
