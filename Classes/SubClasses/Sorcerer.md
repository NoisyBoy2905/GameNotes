Pillar: [[Magic]]
Role: DPS
Range: Ranged

# Playstyle

Uses there wands imbued with magical energy to draw out elements, each wand will have a combination of 3 different elements (One is the main being the strongest, anther will be secondary and the final one will be tertiary), 3 of which are damage focused (Fire, Earth, Lightning) and 3 of which are recovery focused (Ice, Water, Wind). The player will need to switch between absolutely destroying enemies and recovering the mana 

# Abilities
- Auto-attack: Cast a bolt of their wands main element
- GCD abilities: A larger bolt of main element, if ice and fire are together a spell that can explode, if water and lightning are together an AOE stun, if wind and fire are together it applies burn to all enemies
- Off-GCD abilities: something like lucid dreams from FFXIV, 

# Strengths & Weaknesses

They are a glass canon with an insane damage out put but if they get in close they are almost destined to die. The sorcerers are never good with mana seeing as they always run out quickly but if they fully deplete there wont be a chance to come back unless they carry potions 

# Gear

Each wand has 3 different elements, if they use the global magic set it provides a small buff to all the elements but if they get their class specific gear each one will roll a larger bonus to a certain element.
