# Playstyle

Medics think that all life matters even the dungeon enemies so they refuses to fight them but they hate to see there friends come back injured so they go with them to help and heal, but they wont go out without a fight if need they will retaliate with a mixture of syringes 

# Abilities
- Auto-attack: N/A
- GCD abilities: Heal via bandage (can not move for like 2 seconds), Adrenaline shot (buffs allies), if targeted by enemies they can use sedatives on them to stun or slow, healing mist spray can with like pain killers healing allies and enemies 
- Off-GCD abilities: Refill supplies 

# Strengths & Weaknesses

The medics know that this natural phenomenon will keep happening whether or not the parties keep delving into the dungeon's so they just hope they can keep themself and their parties alive within them, they are fragile due to never swinging around a giant blade or wearing heavy armour instead choosing to go light with just a trench coat adjacent

# Gear

When using the Science global gear the medics feel out of there comfort zone due to it being bulkier what they normally wear slowing them down, this changes when they equip their specific gear making them more agile and increasing the ability to heal. They have strong buff that they can apply to there allies and can defend themself if need be but if they get caught while healing it might be over for them
