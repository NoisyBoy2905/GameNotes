Pillar: [[Religion]]
Role: Tank 
Range: Melee

# Playstyle

Can play either sword and shield or run heave blade (double handed great sword), plays up close and person with a couple of AOE attacks to keep enemies agro 

# Abilities
- Auto-attack: Single target slash
- GCD abilities: AOE slash, heavier slash, if shield equipped like a shield bash that stuns, if great sword equipped its like a Spinning slash leaving holy ground beneath them buffing allies with overtime heals and damaging enemies 
- Off-GCD abilities: Taunt (like 30 second cooldown), heal allies, damage reduction spell for like big hit from boss (anti tank buster)

# Strengths & Weaknesses

Its strengths are that it is very tanky, good at controlling larger group of enemies (AOE and Taunts) and can heal themselves. but its weaknesses are that its melee only so ranged unit might cause issue for them and only has 2 damage types so if enemies resist them they are kinda useless

# Gear

If sword and shield is equipped the player gets a safer play style with more defence and CC while if they have the great sword equipped they have lower defence by more AOE to deal with larger groups (also thinking of adding lifesteal to the great sword)
