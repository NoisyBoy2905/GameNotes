# The Truth (private, players never see this page directly)

## The God
An angry god, outcast from his family (the divine). He was severed from them, so he severs worlds from theirs.
- Motive (draft): he is not trying to conquer, he is trying to rebuild a home of his own out of stolen pieces of other worlds.
- He pulls places from other worlds into this one and uses their minerals and resources to grow the dungeons and strengthen the enemies.
- He revives enemies that die inside his dungeons.
- His anger grows over time, which is why dungeons get longer and harder.

## What "Severance" means
- Places are severed from the worlds they belong to.
- The god was severed from his family.
- Players sever dungeons from him.

## Dungeon rules
- Every dungeon is a stolen piece of another world.
- Mutated animals and rogue robots are symptoms of being torn away from home.
- While connected to the god, a dungeon revives its dead enemies.
- Cut off = reclaimed: the dungeon stops reviving enemies and slowly turns into ordinary land. Cities build outposts there.
- Dungeon 3's recon clones show the god is now watching the players.

## What each pillar gets right and wrong
| Pillar | Believes | Right about | Wrong about |
|---|---|---|---|
| Science | Ancient halls pushed up by tectonic plates | Resources and minerals are being drained | The cause is natural |
| Magic | Another world is leaking in | The mechanism (other worlds, torn through) | That it is an accident |
| Religion | Divine punishment for Magic's sin and Science's pride | A god is behind it | That their god sent it, or that it is a punishment |

## Religion and the god (draft)
Religion does not worship him. Their god is the family that cast him out. So Religion is right that it is divine, but it is an exile's revenge, not a punishment.

## The cult (optional, undecided)
A cult that worships the god. It could be a second reason he keeps growing, or a late-game enemy faction. Keep it hidden until later.

## Reveal ladder
1. Dungeon 1: something here does not belong (the river, the animals).
2. Dungeon 2: it came from somewhere else (technology, a severed link).
3. Dungeon 3: someone is watching (recon clones).
4. Later: who the god is and what he lost.

## Open questions
- What exactly did the god lose, and who exiled him?
- Can he be defeated, reconciled with, or both?
- Is the cult real?
