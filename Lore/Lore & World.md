
# The World

Each Class ([[Science]], [[Magic]], [[Religion]]) is separated by their vastly different beliefs, they all talk with each other and trade but never can agree on anything.

# The Dungeons

Over what is thought to be 2 millennium ago the dungeons started appearing, at first they were small and barely contained any enemies but as time went along they started growing in size and the enemies started growing in strength, at first a person could go in by themself and come out unharmed now a party of 4 can go in and barely make it out alive. The dungeons have respawning enemies but they can be cut off from the god, severing his connection to it

# Theories 

- Science believes it is the halls of an ancient civilisation being pushed up by the earths tectonic plates 
- Magic thinks its the world fracturing and another one leaking in
- Religion think it is a punishment from the divine, for all the sins committed by the other pillars many millennium ago

# Why they fight together

They fight together out of necessity due to the growing strength of the dungeons and how not one pillar alone could take it out due to the vastly different styles

# The Truth

There is an angry god (outcast in family maybe still undecided) and he is pulling bits of other worlds into theirs and using the worlds minerals and resources to make the dungeons bigger and strengthen the enemies
